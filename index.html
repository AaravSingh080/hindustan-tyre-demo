Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about buying tyres.

What types of tyres are available at Hindustan Tyre Agency?

We offer a wide range of tyres for cars, SUVs, motorcycles, trucks, buses, agricultural vehicles, and commercial fleets. Our inventory includes leading brands and multiple size options to suit different vehicle requirements.

How do I choose the right tyre for my vehicle?

Our tyre experts can help you select the correct tyre based on your vehicle make, model, driving conditions, and budget. You can also refer to your vehicle owner's manual or contact us for personalized recommendations.

Do you provide tyre fitting and wheel balancing services?

Yes, Hindustan Tyre Agency offers professional tyre fitting, wheel balancing, and related services to ensure optimal performance, safety, and tyre longevity.

Are genuine branded tyres available at Hindustan Tyre Agency?

Absolutely. We source tyres directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers to ensure that every tyre sold is genuine, high-quality, and backed by the manufacturer's warranty.

How often should I replace my vehicle's tyres?

Tyre replacement depends on tread wear, age, driving habits, and road conditions. Generally, tyres should be inspected regularly and replaced when the tread depth reaches the recommended limit or if there are signs of damage or excessive wear.
